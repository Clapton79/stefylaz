We’ll celebrate aboard the **Európa Hajó**, a Budapest event boat on the Danube. For more information about the vessel and its facilities, visit the [official Európa Hajó website](https://europahajo.hu/en/home/).

### Evening schedule

| Time | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| 17:30 | Arrival and welcome. If possible, we’ll show a welcome video and offer a drink in the bar area before inviting guests to the deck. |
| 17:45–18:30 | Ceremony and speeches on the upper deck. The boat may begin cruising toward the end of the ceremony. |
| 18:15–20:00 | Cruising and photos, with Spanish jamón, cheese, gildas, banderillas and drinks. |
| 20:00–22:00 | Buffet dinner, speeches and games. |
| 22:00–23:30 | Live music with the band. |
| 23:30–01:30 | Midnight dinner and dancing continue. |
