title: "Exploring BodaPest"
---

While you are here for our cruise wedding, we highly recommend setting coordinates for these iconic locations around town:

* **The Hungarian Parliament Building:** An architectural marvel visible right from the Danube stream.
* **Buda Castle & Fisherman's Bastion:** Scale the hill for a sweeping panoramic view over the entire city.
* **Széchenyi Thermal Bath:** The ultimate location to relax your gears and unwind before the big celebration.
